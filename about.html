About Joss Sweet Crumbs Bakery

Serving Chiawelo and the wider Soweto community since 9 March 2021.

Our Soweto story

Joss Sweet Crumbs Bakery was established on 9 March 2021 in Chiawelo, Soweto. Located at 2526 Chiawelo on Chris Hani Road, the bakery aims to make fresh and affordable baked goods available to the local community.

Our products include fresh bread, birthday and event cakes, scones, muffins and cupcakes. We also take enquiries for catering platters and bulk bread supply.

Our mission

To provide the Soweto community with freshly baked and affordable baked goods every day from 05:00 to 22:00 with warm, local hospitality.

Our vision

To be Soweto’s preferred choice for birthday cakes, daily bread and community event catering.

Our values

Freshness, affordability, friendly service, care and commitment to our community.
